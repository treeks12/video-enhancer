Independent demonstrations. No affiliation, sponsorship or endorsement is claimed.

Image processing: each source is cropped to 16:9 and resampled to a 640x360 demo input.
Before: native bilinear enlargement to 1280x720. After: released RAVU-lite + RCAS shaders, sharpness 85%.
No extra blur, manual retouching, original high-resolution pixels or generated detail is added to the enhanced output.
Promotional images and video-preview images are illustrative samples, not captures of playing video.

The popup is the published 0.0.72 Store interface with example readings. It is not a benchmark.

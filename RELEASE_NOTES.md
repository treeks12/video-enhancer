New: Framegen neural interpolation, now built in

A new interpolation option is available in Video Enhancer: Framegen v7-small. Choose Framegen in the interpolation engine selector to try a different neural model for smoother motion.

- Framegen and IFRNet-S are both included: install the extension and choose your engine, with no separate model download.
- One extension for everyone: the Personal and Store editions have been unified.
- IFRNet-S remains the default; Framegen is an experimental alternative and can be more demanding or less stable. Existing engine preferences are preserved.

Framegen is developed by Monzik Allen / the Framegen project. Its runtime is MIT licensed; its original bundled weights are licensed for non-commercial research and personal use. Full credits and licenses are available under Models in the popup.

Performance and visual artifacts vary with the GPU, browser and source video.
